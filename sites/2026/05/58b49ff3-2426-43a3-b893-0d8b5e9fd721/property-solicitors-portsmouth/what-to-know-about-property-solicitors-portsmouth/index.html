What this includes

Property solicitors in Portsmouth provide support for a wide range of property transactions and legal matters. This includes buying and selling residential or commercial property, handling lease agreements, managing property disputes, and guiding clients through the conveyancing process. They also offer advice on remortgaging, transfer of equity, and lease extensions.

  • Residential and commercial conveyancing
  • Lease agreements and extensions
  • Property disputes
  • Remortgaging and transfer of equity

Frequently asked questions

What does a property solicitor in Portsmouth do? +

A property solicitor handles the legal aspects of buying, selling, or leasing property, including conveyancing, drafting contracts, and resolving disputes.

How long does the conveyancing process take? +

The conveyancing process typically takes between 8 to 12 weeks, depending on the complexity of the transaction and the parties involved.

Do I need a solicitor for remortgaging my property? +

Yes, a solicitor is required to handle the legal paperwork and liaise with your lender during the remortgage process.

Can you help with lease extensions in Portsmouth? +

Yes, property solicitors can advise and manage the process for lease extensions, including negotiations and legal documentation.

What information do I need to provide to start the process? +

You will need to provide identification, details of the property, and any relevant documents such as mortgage offers or current leases.
