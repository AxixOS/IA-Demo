Lease Extension Cost FAQs

What are the main costs involved in a lease extension? +

You can expect solicitor fees, valuation or surveyor costs, Land Registry charges, and possibly freeholder’s legal and valuation fees.

How much do lease extension solicitors in Portsmouth typically charge? +

Fees vary depending on the complexity of your case and the property value. Contact us for a tailored estimate.

Are there any hidden charges I should be aware of? +

We provide a clear breakdown of all expected costs upfront. Any additional charges will be discussed before proceeding.

Do I need to pay for the freeholder’s costs? +

Usually, you are responsible for covering your freeholder’s reasonable legal and valuation costs as part of the process.

How can I get a detailed cost estimate for my lease extension? +

Get in touch with our team for a personalised quote based on your lease and requirements.
